**Every solstice, we invite friends over to build a big "effigy" out of scrap wood and light it on fire at sunset.**
Attendees are encouraged to bring stuff to add into the fire. It can be sentiments or intentions written down, sentimental objects to let go of, durable objects to "cleanse" or patina, or just to burn for fun. Objects that have gone in so far include:
	- family "heirlooms" with unwanted emotional baggage
	- animal bones
	- notes/letters
	- pottery
	- tungsten cube
	- bronze sculpture
	- an axe
	- a baby doll
	- beer can
	- hand puppet
	- invasive plants

During cleanup, some charcoal is collected from each fire to be added to the next one. Thus there is a direct physical connection between each fire, going back to the first one in December 2024.
