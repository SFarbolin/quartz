# Disassembly notes
#### Small parts and screws (trailing number indicates slotted screw size)
A - black thick pan head screw - 150-3
B - large silver pan head short screw - 300-2
C - large silver pan head short small diameter screw - 210-2
D - small silver pan head screw - 210-2
E - silver thick pan head screw - 180-2
F - large silver thick pan head shoulder bolt - 240-2
G - silver extra long set screw - 150-2
H - small silver pan head screw - 150-2
I - large black thick pan head screw - 240-4
J - black C- 210-2
K - large diameter long thick head silver screw - 240-5
L - large long silver pan head screw, ~half threaded - 300-2
M - L shaped silver bracket
N - large longish silver pan head screw, ~3/4 threaded - 300-2
O - black B - 300-2
