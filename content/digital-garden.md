title: Digital Garden

A *digital garden* is a kind of personal site where pages are added and updates in a organic, nonlinear way.

As opposed to something like a blog or social media timeline with new posts at the top and older content increasingly pushed down, this site is more like a [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki).

It's a place to wander around in! I'll do my best to make it a fun, interested place to spend time exploring!

If you're curious about the technical details of how I made this site, take a look at [[how-this-site-works|How this site works]].
