I would differentiate "doodling" from "drawing" in that doodling has no goal other than to doodle. If you have a plan going in, it's not a doodle.
Personally, I find that I'm usually much happier with the results of a "just start drawing and see what happens" doodle than if I try to imagine something and then draw it.

Doodles are [[play|playful]].

I love doodling. Here's some of mine.
